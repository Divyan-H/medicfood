Privacy Policy — MedicFood

Effective date: October 25, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the MedicFood mobile application ("Application") developed and provided by MedicFood ("Service Provider"). The Application is provided as a free service and is available "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects certain information when it is downloaded and used. This may include:

  • Device information (for example IP address, operating system, pages visited in the Application, timestamps, and duration of use).
  • User-provided medical information such as medicine names, dosage schedules, prescription images, and reminder details. This information is used to enable the Application's core medication-management features.
  • Personally identifiable information (for example, email address) to facilitate communication and account management.

The Application does not collect or store precise geographic location data.

Data Use and Purpose

MedicFood uses collected information to provide, maintain, and improve the Application. Typical uses include:

  • Generating personalized medication schedules and smart reminders.
  • Tracking medication adherence and enabling caretaker access/monitoring.
  • Maintaining operational security and preventing unauthorized access or misuse.

All information is stored securely and treated as confidential. Data is not sold, rented, or shared for marketing or advertising purposes.

Third-Party Access

Aggregated, anonymized data may be shared with trusted third parties for analytics and technical improvements. The Application uses third-party services which have their own privacy practices — for example, Google Play Services and Google Analytics for Firebase.

The Service Provider may disclose data if required by law, or to protect rights and safety, or to trusted providers who operate under strict confidentiality obligations.

Data Retention

The Service Provider retains user-provided data while the Application is in use and for a reasonable period thereafter to ensure continued functionality and reliability. Users may request deletion of their data at any time by contacting medicfood.srmist@gmail.com or by submitting an account-deletion request via the form below.

Opt-Out Rights

To stop data collection, uninstall the Application using the standard uninstall process for your device or app marketplace.

Security

MedicFood uses industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical protections to safeguard user data. Medical and personal data are encrypted and stored in secure databases.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If such data is discovered, it will be deleted immediately upon verification. Parents or guardians may contact medicfood.srmist@gmail.com for assistance.
